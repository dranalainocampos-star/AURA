Insurance & Out-of-Pocket Guidelines

Transparency regarding outpatient diagnostics, billing workflows, and provider criteria.

Are specialized advanced scans covered under general PPO insurance? +
Many premium commercial PPO networks offer partial or conditional reimbursement for necessary imaging modalities (like CT Angiography or diagnostic MRIs). Our internal coding department furnishes full itemized superbills to facilitate your claims coordination directly with payers.
Can I self-refer for advanced genomic or cardiac mapping scans? +
Yes. Aura operates an independent diagnostic facility. Our on-staff clinical director evaluates intake history during consultation windows to sign all necessary prescription orders for tracking markers inside our facility labs.
What is the turnaround time for multi-omic genetic panels? +
Standard clinical biomarker screenings process within 3 to 5 business days. Deeper whole-genome sequencing protocols require a specialized 3-week analytical timeline via our cellular sequencing labs before your comprehensive review session.
